Your car gets washed while you get on with your day.

Drop.

Pull into a KULU bay and hand us the keys. Book in the app or just walk up.

2 minutes

Shop.

Off you go. We steam clean your car, inside and out, while you’re at the shops, the gym or home.

30–60 minutes

Collect.

We message you when it’s ready. Pick up your keys and a clean car.

Done

We look after your car like it’s our own.

Steam, not hosepipes

About 3 to 5 litres a car, with a recycling tank. Good for your paint and for the dam levels.

Insured while it’s with us

From the moment you hand over the keys until you drive away.

Our own team

Trained by us, in KULU uniform, working to the same checklist every day.

No surprises at the till

You see the price before we start.

Oh, and there’s coffee.

Our bigger sites have a coffee bar right by the wash bays. Have one while we finish, or take one for the road.

KULU Send. Larger sites also have parcel lockers, so your deliveries wait with your car.

Partner with us

Bring KULU to your estate or centre.

We fund, build, staff and run the site. You get a sought-after amenity and a new income stream from space you already have.

For estates

  • A resident amenity on the doorstep
  • Designed to fit estate guidelines: timber, green walls, landscaping
  • Staffed, insured and secure
  • Water-wise, with off-grid options

For shopping centres

  • Shoppers stay longer
  • Income from idle bays
  • No building works
  • Fully run by us

How we work with you

  1. 01

    Site visit

    We walk your site and agree the best spot.

  2. 02

    Layout proposal

    A tailored design and finish for you.

  3. 03

    Agreement

    Simple terms: a share of turnover or a fixed rent.

  4. 04

    Install

    Modules arrive ready, or we build permanently.

  5. 05

    Open

    Launch marketing with you and your residents or tenants.

What we need from you

  • A pad of parking bays
  • A metered power connection, or we run off-grid
  • Approval for the canopy, signage and bay paint
  • A water point and drain if available (or we bring tanks)

What we take care of

  • Staff, training, uniforms and daily checklists
  • Insurance, including cars in our care
  • Branding, cleaning and site upkeep
  • Bookings, payments and customer service in the KULU app
