Quick answers.

How long does it take?

Handing over your keys takes about 2 minutes. Your car is usually ready in 30 to 60 minutes.

Do I have to book?

No. Booking in the app guarantees your slot, but you can also just walk up.

Is my car insured while you have it?

Yes. From the moment you hand over the keys until you drive away.

How much does it cost?

You see the price in the app, or at the bay, before we start.

What about water?

Our water recycling system collects, filters and reuses wash water on site.
