The idea

Drop the keys. Get on with the day.

Customers book in the KULU App, hand over their keys and get on with their day: shopping, work, the gym, anything. They come back to a clean car.

The KULU App

The whole visit, in their pocket.

From booking to collection, the app brings shoppers back to the centre on a schedule, not by chance.

Bookings

Pick a store, a wash and a time slot, add the car by its plate and pay in the app. Walk-ups go on the same job board.

Live tracking

Customers see when the wash starts and when the car is ready, so they shop without watching the clock.

Memberships

Monthly plans cover every car on an account, with washes shared across the family. Members return every month.

K Loyalty

Every wash earns a stamp tied to the car’s plate: a free drink at four, a free wash at eight.

Safe hands

Each car is photographed before and after, and a key code is checked at collection.

QR booking

Posters at the store and around the centre let shoppers scan and book on the spot.

Coffee · Optional on M and L

A wash becomes a place to stop.

Coffee is an option on every KULU M and KULU L. The hatch is always built in, so a store can open with coffee or add it later, serving barista drinks while customers wait.

Order coffee in the app alongside the wash, or at the hatch.

Each store’s menu shows only the drinks that store serves.

Wash stamps earn free drinks, linking both sides of the business.

KULU S is car wash only and can be upgraded later.
