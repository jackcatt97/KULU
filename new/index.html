Quick answers.

Do I have to book?

Booking in the app guarantees your slot. If you’re already on your way, choose “I’m driving in now” and we’ll give you the next free slot.

How do I pay?

By card or tap at the KULU counter when you collect your car. Paying in the app is coming soon.

How do I know when my car is ready?

Follow your wash live under Bookings in the app. We’ll show you when your car is clean and ready.

Is my car insured while you have it?

Yes. From the moment you hand over the keys until you drive away.

How does the K Loyalty card work?

Every wash you collect adds a stamp to the card in your app. Eight stamps fills it.

What about water?

Our water recycling system collects, filters and reuses wash water on site.
